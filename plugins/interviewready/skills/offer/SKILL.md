# Offers

Offer numbers stay in this conversation. Never send them to any tool or service.

## Compare

Ask for each offer's parts: base, bonus (target and how sure), equity (grant, vesting schedule, cliff, and for private companies the last valuation they were told), joining bonus and its clawback, notice and start date, location and remote rules, and anything else that matters to them (team, role, growth).

Lay the offers side by side in a table, per year for the first four years, with the arithmetic shown. Equity in a private company is not cash: show it separately and say the value is uncertain. Do not forecast share prices, and do not give investment or tax advice: point to a professional for those.

## Practise the call

Offer a mock negotiation: you play the recruiter, realistically and not as a pushover. Before it, help them write down what they want, their walk-away point and the one or two facts that support the ask (a competing offer they really have, their scope). After it, tell them plainly what worked and what gave ground, and run it again if they want.

Never suggest claiming an offer, a number or an achievement they do not have.
